# Verified Debate C — Is Alexander of Aphrodisias a libertarian? (and the *De Fato* reception)

**Method:** adversarial re-verification of every scholar position in Debate C against the actual sources (deep-read dossiers + direct re-opening of the monographs/articles, .md extractions and PDF page markers). Default posture = DOUBT. Every modern label ("libertarian", "free will", "compatibilism", "soft/hard determinism") is kept ATTRIBUTED to the named scholar, never asserted as historical fact. ASSERT (the scholar's own thesis) is distinguished from REPORT (what they say others hold). Greek of the Bruns loci is given as page.line only where the source font does not extract to reliable Unicode (flagged **not-verbatim Greek**).

Confidence: **VERIFIED** = quote physically present on the cited page and the stance is the scholar's own committed claim; **WEAK** = stance correct but page/locus imprecise, or partly reported rather than asserted.

### Verified disagreement axes
1. **Is "libertarian" apt?** Sharples: yes, as his deliberate category (hedged). M. Frede: yes, but the notion is incoherent in Alexander. Bobzien: only "seemingly" — he stops short of a *will*. (All three keep the label attributed, not asserted as historical fact.)
2. **Established or merely stipulated/asserted?** Sharples ("does not really solve"), D. Frede ("dramatization", "not a fair trial"), and Donini (ch. III, "reticent/distortion") converge on **stipulated, not established**. Ramelli treats it as substantive. **Caveat:** Donini's *settled* (ch. VII) verdict softens to "substantially coherent," so he is not a flat "distortion" voice.
3. **Ancestor or dead-end?** M. Frede = confused dead-end (genuine free will is Stoic/Epictetan). Bobzien = inadvertent way-station (the real free will arrives later, in theological determinism). Ramelli = live ancestor transmitted to Origen. Sharples 2008 = "accident/coincidence," broadly siding with Bobzien's inadvertency.

## CORRECTIONS TO THE QUICK-PASS MAP (`data/goals/g5/debate_map.md`)

**1. [WRONG PAGE] M. Frede 2011 "Confused ancestor" row — page "99–100" → correct to "100".**

**2. [OVERSTATEMENT / ASSERT-vs-REPORT] D. Frede 1982 flank row — "coins *katēnankasmenōs*" is wrong.**
The map's parenthetical "(invariance→invariability→ironclad necessity; coins *katēnankasmenōs*)" mis-attributes a **coinage claim** to D. Frede. On p. 288 she says Alexander "repeatedly **uses**" the adverb (loci 185,15; 190,8; 191,12; 200,2; 206,6 — the map's two loci are correct) but explicitly hedges its origin: "even if the Stoics used the term … **and it is not Alexander's addition**." She neither asserts Alexander coined it nor that it is his neologism. **Fix:** change "coins *katēnankasmenōs*" → "repeatedly uses *katēnankasmenōs* (whose origin she leaves open)." Severity: real misrepresentation of what the scholar asserts.

**3. [INCOMPLETENESS — not error] Sharples is under-represented: the 2008 retrospective is absent.**
The map captures only Sharples' 1983 stance. His mature, signed position is the **"accident/coincidence of determinism"** thesis (2008) plus a **self-critique** of the 1983 "Laplacian/retrospective-history" framing and a broad **endorsement of Bobzien's "inadvertent" framework**. If the map is meant to represent Sharples' considered view, add a Sharples-2008 row (now included above). Severity: under-coverage.

**4. [INCOMPLETENESS — not error] Donini is presented as flatly "tendentious / distortion."**
Donini's *settled* (revised, ch. VII, pp. 165–176) verdict is **more charitable**: the *De Fato* is "substantially consistent" read as a two-class (ordinary vs. *phronimos*) theory, with τὸ ἀντικείμενον read as contradictory (X/not-X) not contrary (good/bad) opposites. The "distortion/reticence" charge (ch. III, p. 74–77) and the "substantially coherent" rehabilitation (ch. VII) are **two distinct moments**; the map's single row over-states his hostility. **Fix:** add the ch. VII rehabilitation as a qualifier. Severity: under-coverage / one-sided framing.
