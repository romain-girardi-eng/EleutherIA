# Verified FOR/AGAINST map — Debate D: Semantics and transmission of *eph' hēmin* and *autexousion*

**Method.** Adversarial re-verification of every Debate-D scholar position in the quick-pass map (`data/goals/g5/debate_map.md`, §"DEBATE D") against the actual sources. Default to doubt. Each claim re-opened in the deep-read dossiers (`read_eliasson_2008.md`, `read_bobzien_2001.md`, `read_karamanolis_2021.md`, `read_amand_1973.md`) AND re-checked against the underlying `.md`/`.txt`/PDF where a page-anchor was in question. Confidence = **VERIFIED** (anchor physically located in the source file at the cited page/note and the scholar genuinely asserts the mapped claim) or **WEAK** (claim mapped but the cited *page/edition* could not be confirmed from the dossier's source files in this repo — typically because the row cites a *different* work, e.g. Bobzien 1998 *Phronesis* article, of which no deep dossier or extraction exists here).

**Integrity.** All modern labels ("free will", "libertarian", "compatibilism", "invention of the will", "two-sided/one-sided") are ATTRIBUTED to the named scholar, never asserted as historical fact. ASSERT vs REPORT distinguished per scholar. Greek transcribed only where physically present in the source file; Eliasson `.md` strips diacritics (accent-normalised, flagged), Bobzien `.txt` is OCR-garbled (intended reading in brackets).

**Verified disagreement line:** Bobzien vs Frede on **when** the first concept of free will arises — **Alexander** (Bobzien) vs **Epictetus** (Frede). Eliasson stands apart: he refuses the whole "when was free will invented" framing as not his question (deflationary), and he *endorses* Bobzien on Chrysippus while *breaking* with her on Alexander (Debate C, outside this point).

## Corrections to the quick-pass map

Listed by severity. Re-verification found the Debate-D map **substantively sound** — no misattributions and no ASSERT-vs-REPORT confusions in the cells that carry dossiers. The defects are citation-precision and one structural gap.

2. **[Work/pagination mismatch — confirmed] Bobzien Debate D (ii) row labeled "Bobzien 1998", pages "165–167, 173–175".** These page numbers are from **Bobzien 1998, "The Inadvertent Conception and Late Birth of the Free-Will Problem", *Phronesis* 43** — a *different work* from the 2001 monograph that the deep-read dossier covers. The 2001 monograph (the only Bobzien text extracted in the repo) carries the SAME substance but at different loci: *autexousion*→synonymous at **p.330**, Stoic *eleutheria* never linked to *eph'hēmin* at **p.341**, Justin "earliest surviving evidence" at **p.345**. Recommend either (a) re-tag the substantiated anchors to the 2001 monograph pages above (VERIFIED), or (b) flag the 1998 row as not-in-repo. Low-medium severity (the claim is true; only the provenance is unverified here).

3. **[Same as #2, Point (i)] Bobzien "Aristotle two-sided ≠ indeterminist", pp.142–143.** Also Bobzien **1998** pagination, not in the repo extraction. The 2001 monograph supports the substance (ch.6) but at different pages. Mark **WEAK** pending the 1998 article. Low severity.
