  probabilist/pragmatist, *not* dogmatic; the ≥3/6 rule; the theme + 5 reconstructed arguments; Origen / Eusebius /

## CORRECTIONS TO THE QUICK-PASS

The quick-pass map (`debate_map.md`) **never built a Debate E**, so there is no false FOR/AGAINST cell to correct;
the corrections below concern (a) the *deep dossier* `read_amand_1973.md` (which I adversarially re-checked against the
book), and (b) the quick-pass map's *adjacent* handling of the same material.

**§0 — "Amand–Carneades precedent" as method label — NO ERROR (confirmed fair).** `arbitrable_debates.md` l. 3 and
`arbitration_hen_pote_ote_ouk_en.md` l. 4 invoke Amand only as the model of "a contested attribution decided by
checking what the ancient text actually says". This is an accurate distillation of Amand's self-described "méthode
strictement philologique et historique" (p. X) and the ≥3/6 *règle de fer* (p. 573). **Keep.**

**§1 — Witness #1 (Philo) locus overstated in the dossier.** `read_amand_1973.md` (l. 45) lists witness #1 as
**"Philon, *Περὶ προνοίας* I,77-88"**. The Conclusion's actual witness-list locus (book p. 571) is
**"Περὶ προνοίας, I, 79-83"** (ed. Aucher I, 36-39). The "I,77-88" range appears in the body discussion but is **not**
the canonical témoin locus. → **Correct the dossier témoin #1 to I,79-83.** (Severity: minor; wrong sub-range.)

**§2 — Witness #2 (Alexander) — silent emendation not flagged.** The dossier lists witness #2 as Alexander
***Περὶ εἱμαρμένης (De fato)* 16-20** — which is **substantively correct** (the book's TOC titles the work
"Le Περὶ εἱμαρμένης d'Alexandre d'Aphrodise" and "chapitres 16 à 20"; Bruns 186-191 = *De Fato*). BUT the printed/OCR
witness-list line (book p. 571, OCR l. 29354) literally reads **"ALEXANDRE D'APHRODISE, Περὶ προνοίας, 16-20"** — an
OCR/typesetting slip (Περὶ προνοίας dittographed from witness #1's line). The dossier silently "corrected" this to
*De Fato* without noting the source line is corrupt. → **The dossier's reading is right; flag that it is a silent
emendation of a corrupt source line.** (Severity: minor; the dossier is *more* accurate than the literal text, but the
emendation should be transparent.)

**§3 — Nemesius chapter "85" propagated an OCR typo.** `read_amand_1973.md` Claim 4 cites the piety/prayer cross-ref
as "**Nemesius *De nat. hom.* 85**". Amand consistently cites **chapter 35** ("Traité de la nature de l'homme, 35",
PG 40, 741C; OCR ll. 20777, 29517, 29566); the single "85" (OCR l. 29734) is a 3→8 OCR digit error. *De nat. hom.* has
~43 chapters, so 85 is impossible. → **Correct the dossier to ch. 35.** (Severity: minor; OCR typo carried through.)

**§4 — Origen Philocalie line-range — NO ERROR (re-confirmed).** The dossier cites Origen *Philocalie* 23,1 ="ed.
Robinson p. 188, ll. 16-29" for the transcribed Greek "Πυθοίμεθα δ᾽ ἂν αὐτῶν…". I initially suspected a clash with the
book's "p. 188, l. 5-16" footnote — but that footnote (OCR l. 16902) belongs to a **different** quotation ("Καὶ εἰ τὰ
ἀκόλουθά τις…"). The transcribed passage's own footnote (OCR l. 16945) reads exactly "p. 188, l. 16-29". **Dossier
correct; keep.**

**§5 — Amand vs Ramelli must not be conflated.** This is the one substantive risk for any future Debate-E map. The
quick-pass already flags it correctly in `read_amand_1973.md` (l. 97) and `arbitrable_debates.md` (l. 56): **Amand
treats Alexander as a *parallel witness* to the lost Carneadean stock, never as the *source* of Origen**; he is
explicit (p. 586) that he cannot even decide whether Alexander reproduces Carneades verbatim. **Ramelli's** thesis
(`positions_alexander.jsonl`) is the opposite filiation — **Alexander → Origen direct inspiration**. Amand's framework
would route *both* Alexander and Origen back to Carneades/Clitomachus (via Cicero/Antiochus and Philo's Jewish
apologetic). → **If a Debate-E map ever cites Amand *in support of* Ramelli's Alexander→Origen priority, that is a
misattribution.** They are neighbouring, not aligned. (Severity: would-be major if conflated; currently NOT conflated
in the corpus — flagged pre-emptively.)

**§6 — No Carneades-transmission position records exist.** `positions_*.jsonl` contain **zero** dedicated records for
the Carneadean anti-astrology / anti-fatalist transmission thesis. The only Carneades tokens are inside M. Frede's
Alexander record (REPORTING Carneades' unforced-assent notion) and the Ramelli record. → A future build should add an
**Amand 1945/1973** position record so Debate E has a first-class witness, and a **`precedes`/`reuses_argument`** edge
chain Carneades → {Philo, Alexander, Firmicus, Eusebius, Origen, Gregory, Chrysostom, Nemesius} grounded on the six
témoins. (Severity: gap, not error.)
