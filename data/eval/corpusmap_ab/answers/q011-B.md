Aristotle's analysis in NE III.1-5 is the base on which the Stoics built. An action is involuntary under compulsion (bia, the archē wholly external) or ignorance of particulars. It is voluntary when its archē is in the agent, who knows the particulars. Prohairesis concerns what is "up to us", and at 1113b7-8 acting and not acting are both eph' hēmin. Responsibility is tied to being the internal source of the action [argument_aristotles_voluntary_action_argument_eph_hemin_e5dd9188; concept_hekousion_voluntary_aristotle_a1b2c3d4]. The same node says the Stoics took eph' hēmin as a technical term for what falls within rational assent, recasting this causal vocabulary in a compatibilist framework. It also says Bobzien reads Aristotle as giving a compatibilist analysis the Stoics inherited. Frede, by contrast, denies he has a libertarian concept of free will [argument_aristotles_voluntary_action_argument_eph_hemin_e5dd9188].

Stoic compatibilism keeps determinism and locates responsibility in assent. Voelke holds that assent is free because its perfect and principal cause is in us [scholarly_argument_voelke_perfect_cause_free_assent]. Koch reads that cause as the individual pneumatic nature of each hegemonikon, which makes Chrysippus a determinist [scholarly_argument_koch_2011_individual_nature_complete_cause]. Brennan reconstructs the response to the cylinder (Cic. De Fato 40-43) as semantic and psychological [scholarly_argument_brennan_chrysippus_semantic_compatibilism]. The entity page notes that the debate is modern and disputed pending primary recollation [debate_stoic_compatibilism].

Alexander of Aphrodisias criticises the Stoics for conflating hekousion with eph' hēmin. On his view, hekousion is shared with animals and does not suffice for responsibility, so rational alternatives are required (Fat. 12-14) [argument_voluntary_vs_eph_hemin]. Labarrière stresses that EN III's analysis is mainly juridical, concerning imputation [scholarly_argument_labarri_re_voluntary_and_involuntary_heko_1]. Whether Aristotle's voluntariness is compatibilist remains unresolved in the corpus; I did not read the primary passages directly.
