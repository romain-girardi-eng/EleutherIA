ANSWER:
The KG frames a patristic debate opposing Christian autexousion to Gnostic cosmic determinism [debate_christian_gnostic_freedom; concept_autexousion_christian_freedom_u1v2w3x4]. Fürst argues that polemic against Marcionite, Valentinian and Basilidean "predestinarian" schools is what led the apologists (Justin, Theophilus, Tatian, Irenaeus, Clement, Tertullian) to make freedom a central polemical issue [argument_furst_2022_irenaeus_against_gnostic_natures].

- Justin: foreknowledge is not causation. Reward and punishment "according to merit" would be meaningless if everything happened by heimarmenē (1 Apol. 43–44). He also says God made angels and humans αὐτεξούσιοι [argument_justin_martyrs_apologetic_argument_for_free_will_45c2dde2; ad0049c4-8fa3-417c-9f02-42a98b0a862f]. This node names Stoic fate as well as "Gnostic necessity" as his targets.
- Tatian rejects being born "according to fate" (γένεσιν τὴν καθ᾿ εἱμαρμένην) [f8ceab87-f393-4ca6-aceb-e28dd1346abe]. The target here is astral or pagan fate, not explicitly Gnostic.
- Irenaeus answers the Valentinian fixed natures (pneumatic/psychic/hylic) with a reductio: if people were good "by nature" rather than by will, their goodness would deserve no credit (Adv. Haer. IV 37,6) [argument_irenaeuss_antignostic_argument_for_free_will_f54fe920; argument_furst_2022_irenaeus_against_gnostic_natures].
- Tertullian argues for free will against Marcion's dualism [argument_tertullians_antimarcionite_argument_for_free_will_f49cad73].
- Origen continues the line. He refutes heretics who read Pharaoh's hardening as proof of lost and saved "natures of souls" (De Princ. III 1,8) [dc6ff8bc-31d6-52a9-a0c8-416df4587c99; a343b50a-6de2-55be-b2d7-80e1c89133e5], and he rebuts the Idle Argument [scholarly_argument_sytsma_anti_determinism_idle_argument_deprinc_iii; 8a7ecc77-2a73-5d39-8470-46b65ff495e0].

Caveat: Löhr sees "Gnostic determinism" as a heresiological stereotype created by Irenaeus, developed by Clement and perfected by Origen. He leaves open whether it describes the Gnostic sources fairly [scholarly_argument_l_hr_gnostic_determinism_as_heresio_0]. No Irenaeus passage text was retrieved from the corpus.
