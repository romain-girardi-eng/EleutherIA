ANSWER:
The KG presents the Christian–Gnostic dispute as a patristic debate opposing human self-determination (autexousion) to cosmic and "natural" determinism [debate_christian_gnostic_freedom; concept_autexousion_christian_freedom_u1v2w3x4]. Fürst argues that the fight against Marcionite, Valentinian and Basilidean predestinarian teaching drove the apologists (Justin, Theophilus, Tatian, Irenaeus, Clement, Tertullian) to make freedom a central polemical issue [argument_furst_2022_irenaeus_against_gnostic_natures].

- Justin: foreknowledge is not causation. Reward and punishment "according to merit" would make no sense if everything happened by heimarmenē. God made angels and humans αὐτεξουσίους [argument_justin_martyrs_apologetic_argument_for_free_will_45c2dde2; 9a8ffeae-8eab-445f-9475-5071fc3590f4].
- Tatian refuses a birth "according to fate" (γένεσιν τὴν καθ᾿ εἱμαρμένην). This is aimed at astral fate, not explicitly at Gnostics [f8ceab87-f393-4ca6-aceb-e28dd1346abe].
- The Greek apologists hold that God created rational beings free, so evil comes from creatures' choice [scholarly_argument_pouderon_free_will_libre_arbitre_in_gre_0].
- Irenaeus, against fixed pneumatic/psychic/hylic natures, argues by reductio (Adv. Haer. IV 37,6): if the good were good "by nature" rather than by will, their goodness would be nothing of their own [argument_irenaeuss_antignostic_argument_for_free_will_f54fe920; argument_furst_2022_irenaeus_against_gnostic_natures].
- Tertullian answers Marcion's dualism with a free-will theodicy [argument_tertullians_antimarcionite_argument_for_free_will_f49cad73].
- Origen attacks "heterodox" who bring in natures that are saved or lost by constitution. He uses Pharaoh's hardening against them [6c5f98e0-6642-53e7-8b27-5cdadb9b4cf2; 176cd86a-4580-4277-a919-5d01d827b3dd; scholarly_argument_sytsma_anti_determinism_idle_argument_deprinc_iii].

Caveat: Löhr treats "Gnostic determinism" as a heresiological cliché created by Irenaeus, developed by Clement and perfected by Origen, and does not judge whether it fits the Gnostic sources [scholarly_argument_l_hr_gnostic_determinism_as_heresio_0]. No Gnostic primary text appeared in my searches.
