Aristotle's analysis (NE III.1-5) makes an action voluntary (hekousion) when its archē lies in the agent, who is neither compelled (bia) nor ignorant of particulars; deliberate choice (prohairesis) concerns what is "up to us" [argument_aristotles_voluntary_action_argument_eph_hemin_e5dd9188][concept_hekousion_voluntary_aristotle_a1b2c3d4]. Its key feature is two-sidedness: "where acting is up to us, so is not acting" (EN 1113b7-8) [1e336c5e-391c-5613-a59d-b83d2bcc5523]; agents are also partly causes of their own character and hence of how the end appears to them (EN 1114b1-12) [28b16b62-34cb-4db0-a445-c778d696cb4e].

Chrysippus instead accepts universal antecedent causation: every proposition is true or false, so everything happens by fate (Cic. Fat. 20-21) [4c862009-c672-509f-b279-4216c11c5826]. He tries to steer between necessitarians and fate-deniers (Fat. 39-40) [093102ae-7069-57f7-9b36-3864bd877fae] by distinguishing principal from auxiliary causes: the external impression is only the proximate trigger of assent, while assent depends on the agent's own nature, like a cylinder's shape (Fat. 42-43) [0338fcd0-3bd0-545f-9587-57231fedd860][SVF II.987: 2b4cf5f8-bcce-59c3-a1fb-62019ae2d930][scholarly_position_sharples_chrysippus_early_compatibilist]. Responsibility thus requires action "through us" according to our nature, not ability to do otherwise (a "Frankfurt-style" reading) [scholarly_position_salles_chrysippus_frankfurt_style]. Epictetus later locates the up-to-us in the use of impressions and assent [00b50707-7fa3-5a84-bcd7-97acb64e12ae].

The Peripatetic Alexander exploits the contrast: the Stoics conflate the merely voluntary (shared with animals) with the up-to-us, which requires being master of doing and not doing via deliberation (De Fato 12-14) [argument_voluntary_vs_eph_hemin][28bc2223-dabd-4bb0-933f-faa0fbcc2ef9][scholarly_argument_frede_alexander_s_critique_of_stoic__2]. Whether Aristotle himself was already compatibilist (Bobzien) or simply lacked libertarian free will (Frede) remains disputed [argument_aristotles_voluntary_action_argument_eph_hemin_e5dd9188].
