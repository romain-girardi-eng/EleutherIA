Boethius states the problem as a dilemma. If God foreknows X, X happens necessarily and freedom, merit and prayer collapse [argument_boethius_foreknowledge_problem][passage_boethius_cons_113]. If instead God judges outcomes as uncertain, his "foreknowledge" is no better than Tiresias's "whatever I say will be or will not" [passage_boethius_cons_112].

The solution has several layers:
(1) Knowledge follows the mode of the knower, not of the thing known [argument_boethius_modes_cognition][37fc51d7-b90b-5c1f-a92a-3a2c40df88d7].
(2) Eternity is "interminabilis vitae tota simul et perfecta possessio", so God's knowledge is "scientia numquam deficientis instantiae", not foreknowledge [passage_boethius_cons_125][concept_aeternitas_boethius_g2h3i4j5]. Seeing something as present adds no necessity to it, just as our seeing does not [passage_boethius_cons_125].
(3) Boethius separates two kinds of necessity: simple necessity and necessity of the condition ("si aliquem ambulare scias, eum ambulare necesse est") [passage_boethius_cons_126][passage_boethius_cons_127].
(4) You can change your plan, but God sees that change too "uno ictu" [passage_boethius_cons_128].

Does this answer the problem? Partly. Step (3) on its own defeats the modal fallacy in V.pr.3: the necessity that follows from knowledge is conditional and leaves the event free "per se". Strictly, step (3) does not need eternity at all. What eternity adds is an answer to the "certainty" and causal-dependence horns: God's knowledge is not caused by future events and is not uncertain [passage_boethius_cons_128][passage_boethius_cons_112]. Boethius himself admits the point is "vix" accessible except to a "divini speculator" [passage_boethius_cons_126].

The KG names critics (Sorabji 1980, Marenbon 2003) but does not report their objections [argument_boethius_foreknowledge_problem]. Later reworkings by Scotus, Ockham and Molina show the issue stayed open [debate_divine_foreknowledge_future_contingents_a7b8c9d0]. The corpus therefore does not establish a verdict on whether the solution succeeds.
