ANSWER:
The KG records Justin as influencing Irenaeus [person_justin_martyr_2c_ce; person_irenaeus_d202], and describes AH IV.37-39 as building on Justin's αὐτεξούσιον (1 Apol. 43-44; Dial. 141) [work_irenaeus_adversus_haereses_book4].

Continuity, visible in the texts:
(1) Both authors say God created angels and humans alike as self-determining. Justin: God "ἐποίησεν αὐτεξουσίους πρὸς δικαιοπραξίαν καὶ ἀγγέλους καὶ ἀνθρώπους" [7b029b3b-d680-4362-a0ea-8ef4b2aef215; 9a8ffeae-8eab-445f-9475-5071fc3590f4; ad0049c4-8fa3-417c-9f02-42a98b0a862f]. Irenaeus: "posuit in homine potestatem electionis, quemadmodum et in angelis" [decb4efc-f086-5663-900a-635c9246b19e].
(2) Both say God does not coerce. Irenaeus has "Βία Θεῷ οὐ πρόσεστιν" [f82703cb-03ab-594d-b492-60c15193a3d0] and "μὴ καταναγκάζοντος αὐτὸν τοῦ Θεοῦ" [4b73889d-a934-57c0-9af1-954c5ba76608].
(3) Both use ἐφ' ἡμῖν and tie praise, blame and reward or punishment to that power [argument_justin_antifatalism; 7c74409a-301c-5e6e-a0a8-ef71a7843cff; a2889e18-9207-5655-8dae-5deffb419d50].

Shifts:
- Different opponent. Justin targets Stoic εἱμαρμένη [argument_justin_martyrs_apologetic_argument_for_free_will_45c2dde2]. Irenaeus targets the Valentinian idea of fixed natures: "εἰ φύσει οἱ μὲν φαῦλοι…" [7c74409a-301c-5e6e-a0a8-ef71a7843cff; argument_irenaeuss_antignostic_argument_for_free_will_f54fe920]. The KG reads this as an echo of Carneades' argument, adapted to Christian use [argument_irenaeus_adv_haer_iv_37_praise_blame_transposed].
- A developmental anthropology. The KG credits the νήπιος doctrine (IV.38) to Theophilus first, not to Justin [concept_nepios_adam_infant_doctrine]. Its "good vs. perfect" framework and pedagogical theodicy are Irenaeus's own extension [concept_agathos_vs_teleios_distinction; concept_pedagogical_theodicy].

Limits: the corpus contains passages only for IV.37.1-4. I found no text from IV.38-39 (apart from the IV.38.1 fragment quoted inside the concept node) and no explicit citation of Justin by Irenaeus. The continuity rests on parallel wording and the KG's influence edge.
