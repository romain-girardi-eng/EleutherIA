Aristotle's criterion (EN III.1) is the voluntary (hekousion): an act is praiseworthy or blameworthy when its origin lies in the agent, who knows the particulars. It is involuntary when it is done by force (bia), with an external origin, or through ignorance. "Mixed" acts done under duress still count as voluntary [argument_voluntary_involuntary_distinction_aristotle_g7h8i9j0]. EN III.5 adds a two-sided eph' hēmin: where acting is up to us, not acting is also up to us [1e336c5e-391c-5613-a59d-b83d2bcc5523]. Frede argues that hekōn/akōn applies even to children and animals, so Aristotle's account of responsibility needs no distinct will or free will [argument_frede_2011_aristotle_no_will_no_free_will].

The Stoics took the internal/external origin contrast and placed it inside universal fate. Chrysippus' cylinder: whoever pushes the cylinder gives it the start of its motion but not its rollability. Likewise an impression triggers assent, but assent stays "in potestate" and moves "suapte vi et natura". Antecedent causes are only proximate, not perfect and principal [0a1bf1ae-9888-4a9e-8da8-9ff3ae4c8f7e; 1812b556-ba17-4407-9998-f0383d158dd2; argument_cylinder_analogy_chrysippus_k1l2m3n4]. On Salles' reading, the Stoics were compatibilists: prior necessitation does not rule out desert, and they rejected hard determinism [scholarly_argument_salles_stoic_compatibilism_1; scholarly_argument_salles_hard_determinism_vs_stoic_comp_2]. Sorabji and Brennan judge that this does not escape necessity [debate_stoic_compatibilism; scholarly_argument_brennan_possibility_definition_cheats].

Alexander turned Aristotle's two-sided formula against the Stoics. On his account, what is up to us is what we are masters of doing and not doing, and determinism abolishes deliberation and therefore eph' hēmin [28bc2223-dabd-4bb0-933f-faa0fbcc2ef9]. Bobzien argues that the "free-will problem" itself came from later antiquity reading Aristotle's prohairesis through Stoic determinism [scholarly_argument_bobzien_origin_of_free_will_problem_in_0].
