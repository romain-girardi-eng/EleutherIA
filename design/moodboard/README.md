# EleutherIA intellectual-atlas moodboard

These references are ingredients, not templates. The target is a light-dominant,
immersive scholarly atlas with a strong WOW effect produced by intellectual
depth, spatial orientation, and evidence clarity.

| Reference | Keep | Transform / avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `01-eleutheria-current.png` | Painted antiquity, terracotta/papyrus/Aegean palette, memorable atlas metaphor | Keep the dark theatrical world for moments of immersion, not the primary research workspace; replace opaque loading with useful progress and orientation |
| `02-stanford-encyclopedia.png` | Immediate authority, search-first entry, editorial restraint, explicit institutional trust | Too static and visually flat for graph exploration; preserve authority while adding spatial intelligence |
| `04-met-timeline.png` | Museum-grade whitespace, decisive hierarchy, image-led entry into a chronology | Avoid generic museum navigation and let sources/relations, not decorative hero media, own the experience |
| `05-connected-papers.png` | One unmistakable primary action and a clear graph mental model | Too generic/SaaS and too centered; EleutherIA needs richer provenance and editorial character |
| `06-google-arts-culture.png` | Immersive visual discovery, confident scale, playful transitions between overview and object | Avoid repetitive card grids, promotional overlays, and entertainment-first interaction |
| `07-kumu.png` | Relationship mapping as a legible promise; complex systems made approachable | Avoid generic network-tool branding and oversimplification of scholarly evidence |
| `08-sefaria-library.png` | Living-library model, source and commentary adjacency, research continuity | Avoid intrusive banners/modals and washed-out hierarchy; make the source surface calmer and more deliberate |
| `09-openalex.png` | Research authority, generous light space, direct search, confident typography | Too sparse for EleutherIA's dense comparative work; combine this clarity with persistent scholarly context |
| `10-eleutheria-graph-loaded.png` | Genuine visual impact, GPU scale, visible search and mode affordances | The full 20k-node view is visually saturated, dark-first, label-poor, and difficult to orient; make this an overview layer, not the default scholarly reading state |

## Synthesis

The desired experience combines SEP/OpenAlex authority, Connected Papers/Kumu
orientation, Sefaria source adjacency, and Met/Arts & Culture immersion. The
distinctive EleutherIA move is the Evidence Thread: a continuous, citable path
through arguments, witnesses, passages, translations, and scholarly disputes.
